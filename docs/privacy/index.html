Server requests

The main epilentio.hr site is delivered through GitHub Pages. GitHub necessarily processes technical connection data to deliver and secure that service, controls its retention, and may process data outside the European Economic Area using the safeguards described in its privacy statement. The links.epilentio.hr site is hosted on infrastructure rented from Hetzner Online GmbH; its security logs may contain an IP address, request time, requested path, response status, and browser information and are retained for up to 14 days. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in reliable and secure website operation.

Optional Matomo analytics

With your consent, the websites load our self-hosted Matomo installation from analytics.marijan.pro. Matomo records page views, followed links, approximate technical and location information, and a referrer with its path removed. Three bytes of each IP address are masked before storage, and masked addresses are used for location enrichment. Matomo is hosted on infrastructure rented from Hetzner Online GmbH.

Raw analytics records are retained for no more than 390 days. Aggregated reports may be retained longer. Matomo cookies may remain for up to 13 months, while your consent choice is stored in your browser for 180 days. Analytics is not loaded unless you select “Allow analytics”. You can withdraw consent at any time through “Cookie settings” in the footer; withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing.

Necessary browser storage

The websites store your theme and analytics choices locally where needed to provide the settings you request. This storage does not itself send analytics data.

Map and external services

The links website does not contact Google Maps until you choose to load the map. Loading it sends technical connection data to Google, which processes data under its own privacy policy. Booking, Airbnb, WhatsApp, Instagram, and other external destinations likewise process data under their own terms after you follow their links.
